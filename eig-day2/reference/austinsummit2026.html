The Details

Questions, answered.

Where exactly is the conference held?

Hyatt Regency Austin
208 Barton Springs Rd, Austin, TX 78704, USA

A premier hotel positioned on Lady Bird Lake, offering scenic downtown views, direct access to the Hike & Bike Trail, and close proximity to downtown entertainment districts.

Exclusive discount rate available for all attendees. Our hospitality team coordinates preferred-rate hotel blocks within walking distance — details shared upon registration.

What's included in the ticket price?

The Client One-Day Pass covers the full Thursday, September 10 program: every session, the Flagship Keynote, Collaboration Lab participation, breakfast, lunch, refreshments throughout, and the Sunset Networking event. The Partner Two-Day Pass adds the Wednesday, September 9 Partners Summit and opening dinner. The Friday, September 11 Master Classes and the golf or social activity are registered separately from both passes.

Is this only for current EIG clients?

The conference prioritizes the invited client community, but we hold a small allocation of seats each year for qualified executives new to EIG. Submit your registration and our team will confirm availability within 48 hours.

What's the refund and transfer policy?

Full refunds are available up to 60 days prior to the event. Within 60 days, tickets may be transferred to another executive at your organization at no cost. For emergency circumstances within 14 days, contact our concierge team.

Can my team attend together? Are there group rates?

Yes. Teams of three or more qualify for preferred pricing, and we can reserve a dedicated Collaboration Lab group for your organization's specific priorities. Contact jdevries@consultingeig.com for group arrangements.

Will the sessions be recorded?

Select keynote and panel content is captured for registered attendees' post-event access. However, the Collaboration Lab and closed-door executive conversations are intentionally off-record — the value of the room requires it.
