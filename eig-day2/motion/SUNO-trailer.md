# Suno prompt: Day 2 On-Demand trailer music

Use **Custom** mode, with the Instrumental switch **off**. The structure below goes in the Lyrics box, and only bracketed tags are used, so there's nothing for Suno to sing.

## Style of Music

```
Cinematic corporate trailer, instrumental, 120 BPM, D major, pulsing synth arpeggios, staccato strings, felt piano, deep sub hits, tight hybrid percussion, confident, premium, modern
```

## Exclude styles (if your Suno version has the field)

```
vocals, choir, four-on-the-floor kick, EDM drop, dubstep, rock guitar, ukulele, whistling, cheesy
```

## Lyrics (structure only)

```
[Instrumental]
[Intro: dark low drone, soft ticking pulse, slow riser]
[Big Impact]
[Main Theme: driving synth arpeggio, staccato strings, tight toms and claps, warm bass]
[Break: sudden stop, one held piano note, heartbeat pulse]
[Build: percussion returns, rising strings, snare roll]
[Climax: full theme, four big accented hits]
[Final Hit: huge impact, held chord, long ring-out]
[End]
```

## Generating

- Make three or four versions and keep the one with the cleanest, most obvious section changes: a clear first impact, a real stop at the break, and a big final hit.
- Any length from 1:30 to 3:00 is fine; I cut the sections to the trailer.
- Download as **WAV** if your plan allows it, otherwise MP3 at the highest quality.
- If Suno sings anything anyway, regenerate. A track with words in it won't work.

## How it fits (for the edit)

| Trailer moment | Time | Wanted from the track |
|---|---|---|
| Cold open, Jose's line | 0–4 s | Intro tension, low and quiet |
| Brand hit | 4.0 s | Big impact, theme starts |
| Slide montage, Aaron | 4–11.5 s | Main theme, groove |
| "If trust is present…" | 11.5 s | Break: sudden near-silence |
| Greg, rebuild | 12–16.5 s | Build |
| Stat hits | 16.5, 17.5, 18.5, 19.5 s | Accented hits |
| Faces, Hosni | 20.5–24.5 s | Climax, snare roll |
| End card | 24.5–30 s | Final hit and ring-out |

All of these land on a 120 BPM grid (one beat is 0.5 s), so sections from a 120 BPM track cut together cleanly. The voices are ducked under the music automatically, and the stat hits can be reinforced with the trailer's own impacts.
